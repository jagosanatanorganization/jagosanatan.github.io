About Us

Jago Sanatan Organisation is a social, cultural, and religious organisation founded in 2023 with the aim of spreading awareness about Sanatan Dharma, preserving cultural values, and serving humanity.

Under the leadership of Kishor Deyari, the organisation works actively in religious education, youth engagement, and humanitarian activities.

Our Activities

  • Bhagavad Gita Competitions
  • Sanatan Dharma Awareness Programs
  • Humanitarian Aid & Social Service
  • Religious & Cultural Events
  • Youth & Volunteer Engagement
